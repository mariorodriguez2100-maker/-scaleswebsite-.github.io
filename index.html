Music is a Language with 6 Parts of Speech

According to Scotty West's system, music is literally a language. Your guitar is a "dumb machine" or typewriter used to type pitch patterns. To be a real player, you must master the 6 main areas of music: Pitch, Rhythm, Timbre, Dynamics, Technique, and Notation.

The 4 Ways to Know Every Pitch Pattern

  • Think (Music Theory): Memorize spelling, grammar, and vocabulary formulas.
  • Read (Notation): Interpret staff notation, tablature, and fretboard grids.
  • Hear (Ear Training): Recognize and sing interval sounds and chord qualities.
  • Speak (Technique): Finger patterns on the fretboard to make your guitar express ideas.

The Musical Number System

Instead of memorizing thousands of variations across 12 distinct alphabetical keys, the number system treats music as if there is only one universal key. The Major Diatonic Scale intervals serve as benchmark numbers (1 to 8) to measure all chords and scale patterns instantly.

Guitar Knowledge Library & Masterclass Reference

1. The 12 Pitch Dictionary & Chromatic Scale

Music consists of 7 basic pitch names (A-B-C-D-E-F-G). Sharps (#) raise pitch by a half-step; flats (b) lower it. The "BE" Rule: All notes have natural sharps except B and E (no B# or E#).

C - C# - D - D# - E - F - F# - G - G# - A - A# - B

2. CAGED System Overview

Any major or minor chord can be played across 5 distinct neck positions using shapes derived from open C, A, G, E, and D chords.

  • C Form: Root on 5th String (A)
  • A Form: Root on 5th String (A)
  • G Form: Root on 6th String (E)
  • E Form: Root on 6th String (E)
  • D Form: Root on 4th String (D)

3. Diatonic Scale Formulas

Major Scale Formula: W - W - H - W - W - W - H (Half-steps strictly between degrees 3-4 and 7-8).

Natural Minor Formula: W - H - W - W - H - W - W.

4. Modal Harmony Reference

The 7 rotational scale centers derived from the Major Scale:

1. Ionian (Major)
2. Dorian (min w/ 6th)
3. Phrygian (min w/ b2)
4. Lydian (Maj w/ #4)
5. Mixolydian (Maj w/ b7)
6. Aeolian (Natural Minor)
7. Locrian (Diminished)
